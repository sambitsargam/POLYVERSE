## Quick Upload Instructions:

1. **Prepare Sample Files**: Create placeholder files for each product type (images, PDFs, videos, etc.)

3. **Upload Process**:

   - Fill in the details from above
   - Set the price
   - Select the appropriate product type
   - Publish to blockchain
